# #115 — the same measurement at real payload sizes and positions

**Status: MEASURED. The recommendation is DECLINE #115 AS SCOPED**, and take the
two representation changes `issue-115-composition.md` named instead.

Every figure in the three preceding documents was taken on a 409,600-byte body
with the scanned field at one of two extremes. Published GitHub webhook payload
examples run 6,032–25,249 bytes with a median near 7,740, and place
`repository` between 0.3% and 75.9% of the way in with a median near 22.1%.
This measures that grid.

Nothing is proposed as normative here and nothing was built.

## The grid

Nine cells, generated by `mksize.py --offset <O> <N>`, which places
`"repository":{` at a REQUESTED byte offset and refuses any offset the structure
cannot express rather than rounding to a nearby one — a generator that quietly
moves the independent variable produces a table whose rows are not the rows it
labels.

Idle control **1,440 KiB**, taken at both ends of the sequence and identical at
both. Every cell answered `202 Accepted` with the repository name extracted
correctly.

## What is measured and what is not

**Measured: totals.** Peak RSS for a complete signed delivery, against an idle
control taken twice.

**NOT measured: the receipt/traversal split at these sizes.** The premise
document decomposed a 409,600-byte run; nothing here reapplies that instrument.
An earlier draft of this document derived a removable saving by scaling the
409,600-byte receipt figure through the origin. **That was withdrawn.**
Near-linearity of a TOTAL cannot establish the slope of one of its COMPONENTS,
and it is the same extrapolation `issue-115-consumer.md` withdrew a crossover
claim for. What packing would save at 7,740 bytes is unmeasured.

**Also corrected from that draft:** it reported a grid whose positions were the
generator's `first`/`mid`/`last` aliases — roughly 0%, 50% and 99% — while
describing them as the observed 0.3%, 22.1% and 75.9%. Those are different
measurements and the table above is the second one.

## Recommendation: DECLINE #115 as scoped

#115 proposes a typed IR, monomorphisation, MLIR/LLVM, layout and calling
convention, optimization and debug info — a project its own text calls "many
sessions", motivated by "what it does not do is run fast" and never by a number.

What four documents of measurement support is narrower:

- The cost is real and large as a RATIO — up to ~0.8 KiB of resident memory per
  byte of body at the far position, ~0.2 at the near one.
- In ABSOLUTE terms at real payload sizes it is **single-digit MiB per request
  at the median, rising to ~19 MiB at the largest sampled size and furthest
  position.** These are per-request transients released by the arena, so a
  receiver's standing cost is that figure times its concurrency, not a leak.
- **99.79% of the dominant column is closure environments, closure boxes and
  boxed `Bool`s** (`issue-115-composition.md`) — and both are REPRESENTATION,
  on the backend's own side of CLAUDE.md's line, binding no other backend and
  adding no proof obligation.

**So the proportionate response is the two changes the composition document
named — not allocating a fresh environment per application, and not boxing
`Bool` — rather than the compiler programme #115 describes.** They target the
same 99.79%, they are backend-local, and neither needs a typed IR to attempt.

Declining #115 as scoped does not discard that work. It separates a bounded,
measured representation fix from an unmeasured compiler project, and the four
documents are the argument for doing the first and not the second.

## What would overturn this

- **A deployment whose concurrency makes single-digit MiB per request
  material.** Nothing here characterises one; this receiver is not deployed.
- **Payloads outside the sampled range.** A `push` carrying hundreds of commits
  exceeds anything measured here; GitHub caps that array at 2,048 entries, and
  nobody has measured at that bound.
- **The two named changes failing to move the number.** They are named on an
  allocation census, not an implementation, and the composition document is
  explicit that naming a direction is not a plan.
